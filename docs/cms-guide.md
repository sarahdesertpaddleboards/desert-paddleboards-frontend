# Updating the website — guide for Sarah & Shellie

You can edit parts of the website yourself (no code) through a simple form-based
editor called **Pages CMS**. When you save, the site rebuilds and your changes
go live in about 2–3 minutes.

## What you can edit today

- **Calendar — City Classes:** the dates/times for the city-run classes
  (Queen Creek, Sedona, Avondale) that aren't booked through FareHarbor.
  *(FareHarbor sessions appear on the site automatically — you don't edit
  those here.)*
- **Homepage Videos:** the "See it in action" video clips.
- **Shop — Products & Prices:** product names, prices, descriptions, images
  and the Stripe payment links.

## How to log in

1. Go to **https://app.pagescms.org**
2. Click **Sign in with GitHub** and log in with your GitHub account.
3. The first time, GitHub will ask you to **authorize Pages CMS** — approve it.
4. Choose the **`desert-paddleboards-frontend`** project.
5. You'll see the three sections above in the left menu. Click one, make your
   changes, and click **Save**.

That's it — the website updates itself a few minutes after you save.

## Adding the city class dates (most common task)

1. Open **Calendar — City Classes**.
2. Find the city (e.g. Queen Creek) or add a new one.
3. Under **Upcoming dates**, click to add a date. Enter the **Date** and the
   **Start time in 24-hour format** — e.g. `20:00` means 8 PM, `19:30` means
   7:30 PM.
4. Save. The new date appears on the homepage calendar and the calendar page.

> Tip: remove dates once they've passed to keep the list tidy (the site hides
> past dates automatically, but it keeps things clean).

## Giving Shellie access

Shellie needs her own (free) GitHub account, then to be added to the project:

1. Shellie creates an account at **https://github.com/signup** (free).
2. Sarah goes to the project on GitHub →
   **Settings → Collaborators → Add people** → enter Shellie's GitHub username.
3. Shellie accepts the email invitation from GitHub.
4. Shellie can now sign in at **https://app.pagescms.org** the same way.

## If you get stuck

- Changes not showing after ~5 minutes? They may still be building — give it a
  little longer, then hard-refresh the page (Cmd-Shift-R).
- Something looks wrong? Don't worry — every change is saved with history and
  can be undone. Message Nick and it can be rolled back.
